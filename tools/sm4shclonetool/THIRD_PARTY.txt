The Aroma plugin (aroma/, 1.5)
- Its source (aroma/src, the Makefile) is the tool's own (MIT). The built plugin, aroma/sm4shclonetool.wps, links:
  the Wii U Plugin System WUPS (wiiu-env/WiiUPluginSystem, GPL-3.0), Aroma's libkernel and libnotifications
  (wiiu-env; see their repositories for their licences), wut (devkitPro, zlib licence) and devkitPPC's newlib (BSD-style
  licences). Because it links WUPS the .wps is distributed under the GPL-3.0's terms; its complete source is in aroma/.
- Cemu's graphic-pack patch format and module checksum were followed from Cemu's source (MPL-2.0); no Cemu code is
  included.
