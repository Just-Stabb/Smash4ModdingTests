# Supamario MSC

`supamario.c`: Supamario's decompiled MSC (first commit: the v5 package's `supamario.mscsb` as
decompiled; a copy of Mario's, without the universal mechanics until `supamario_universal_v1` below). Built by
`tools/supamario_kame_build.py`, which replaces the `KAME_*` names and the Super God Fist's `GF_*` (`--msc-only OUT.mscsb`: only this file, without
the XV2 package, the effect values from `tools/supamario_kame_values.py` `SUPAMARIO_FX`).

## Neutral special: Kamehameha (status 0x152)

`kame_n_*` (end of the file), called from the vanilla status functions: `func_2256` (main: init),
`func_2257` (per frame: `kame_n_loop`), `func_2258` (situation: re-arms the air stall), `func_2279`
(end: the charge effects off). Hold special to charge (the throw frozen at frame 10), release (or
100 frames) to fire article 5 at frame 14 in status 0 / 1 / 2 = the charge level; the firing pose
(frame 16) is held while the beam exists. In the air he floats down while charging and floats while
the beam is out. The fireball
article (0) is unchanged, not generated by this move. Details: `docs/custom-effects.md`, "The move".

## weapons/

- `kamehameha.c`: the Kamehameha projectile (weapon kind 0x18B): fly / hold / finish statuses.

## Kamehameha fix 89 (`build/supamario_kame_fix89.zip`)

Install over fix 88. User (clip): it still only multi-hits a shield fully charged.

- **Cause:** native, not the MSC. A weapon's hit result reaches its singleton's hit method (+0xAC) as bits; the
  base fireball's (0x0320EE2C) removes the weapon on 0x13 and on 0x4 (unless the weapon's byte +0x46 is set),
  and 0x4 is the shield-block result. The beam's own method (`weapon_expand.py` on_hit) caught only 0x13, so a
  shield block went to the base method and the beam was removed before its MSC saw it. Fully charged, the hit
  presumably came in as 0x13 (its damage; its status 3 and the fly-through on the shield ran), hence "only fully charged".
- **Fix:** `extra_kinds.json` kamehameha `"on_hit_bits": "0x17"` (`weapon_expand.py`'s new per-article option):
  a shield block asks for status 3 too, which (fix 85-88) flies on, multi-hitting the shield. One word in the
  RPX: 0x04BB43FC `andi. r0, r5, 0x13` -> `0x17` (`weapon_expand_test.py` checks the method on bit 0x4).
- Changed: `code/cross_f.rpx` only (fix 79's plus that word); the scripts are fix 88's.

### Causes
1. **The flight thread never started.** v3 started it at frame 97 (the first frame after the strike's 8-frame
   freeze) only if `Hit_Check(0, 0, 2)` reported the hit. The clip shows the strike dealt its 11%, so that check
   was false although the hit happened. Most likely timing: the kinds a hit adds to AttackModule `+0xC8` come from
   the attack's per-frame record pass (`0x02C09C0C`: it ORs `1 << kind` of the new records into `+0xC4` /
   `+0xC8`), and on the first frame after the attacker's freeze the script runs before that pass has handled the
   strike's record (the punch's check is 4 frames after its hit, so it always worked). The diag build logs the
   kinds at 97-100 (k 4) to confirm; the fix doesn't depend on it. With no thread, nothing moved the victim, turned
   it, put it down or turned it back.
2. **Nothing else zeroes the rotation.** `Rotation_Set` is `PostureModule(4, ...)`; no status change resets it (the
   clip: the victim back in its idle pose, still tilted). v3 zeroed it only inside the flight thread (on the
   down's first frame and some releases), so any way the hold ended without the flight (this one; Supamario hit or
   grabbed mid-hold; a new dash attack; a second God Fist on another fighter while the first lay down) left the
   last lean on the victim, and the hold's stage collision 0 too (it could fall through the stage).
3. (Not the cause, checked:) threads survive status changes and the motion jump (acmd_ext_cave.py: only the
   object's set-up, `WM_RESET 0x02BF5864`, drops them; the jump only changes the motion rate); `Thread_Start`
   with Flags 1 runs during the attacker's hitlag. My first guess, the strike blocked by the punch's Part 0 hit
   record (rehit 0: `0x02C10BC8` never frees such a record), is ruled out by the clip's 11%; the strike is on
   Part 1 now anyway (a record of its own, `Hit_Log_Clear(1)` before it), which costs nothing.

## Super God Fist v12 (`supamario_god_fist_v12.zip`): a shorter, quieter startup sound, an off-stage catch held in place, XV2's tracking, a faster startup on a combo

v12 is installed over v11 and still uses exe B (`exe_v46i`). The user asked:
- "make Super God Fist's startup sound less loud and end much sooner";
- "if it hits an opponent off stage then it halts Supamario's momentum and stops him from falling until the animation
  is finished";
- partial tracking of an opponent in hitstun, the initial burst only: not behind him, at most 60 degrees up or down,
  horizontally at most 1.5x faster;
- "when the move is done with an opponent in hitstun who is in range (40 units), the startup of Super God Fist should
  be cut in half".

### Startup sound
- **Quieter:** 6 dB down (the tone's volume, -6.0).
- **Shorter:** the waveform is cut at 20 frames, with a linear fade over its last 6 frames (frames 14-20), so it ends
  with the punch rather than at ~130. This is `se_pcm` in the build, and it applies to the label's tone in the bank.
- **Why trimmed rather than stopped:** I did it in the waveform, not with a stop from the sound script. Smash 4's ACMD
  has no command to stop one sound, and a trimmed tone ends the same way whatever happens to the move.
- **What changes:** same label and sound id (0x0CB10091); the soundlabeltable is unchanged.

### An off-stage catch held in place
- **When it applies:** a catch made in the air (situation 2 on the catch's frame, e.g. after running off a ledge or
  tracking up).
- **The MSC pins him:** gf_update stores his X / Y and writes them back every frame up to GF_IASA (92).
- **The game script stops his fall:** at the catch, if Input_Get's SITUATION is 2, it sets
  `Gravity_Set(-1, 0, 2)`. That keeps his own gravity, so a rise slows; sets max fall to 0; and cuts a fall at once.
- **Afterwards:** the hold, the strike, the flight and the flip play in place, and the victim's offsets are relative
  to him. At 92 the move ends in Fall (v10's air path) and he falls normally.
- **Restoring gravity:** Gravity_Set ends by itself on the status change. It also ends on a hit, a ledge grab or a KO,
  and the pin stops with the status. The launched victim goes into DamageFall with its stage collision back and falls
  as before (gf_tick lets it go if it doesn't land within 30 frames).

### Tracking (XV2's), the burst only
- **The target:** the nearest fighter, over player slots 1-8, that is:
  - in hitstun (hitstun left > 0 and a damage status 0x45-0x4C);
  - in front of him (along his facing);
  - within 60 degrees of level (dy^2 <= 3 dx^2).
  
  It is read through the slot position / hitstun / status variables and picked again every frame. He is never in
  front of himself, so he is never the target.
- **When:** only over the dash's burst, motion frames 9-15, where his TransN covers 4.53 a frame (`BURST_SPEED`,
  checked against the motion). Before and after the burst, no tracking.
- **How far:** his travel is aimed at the target. Its horizontal part is scaled by k = (the distance to it less the
  catch's 11.5) / (the burst's way left), kept between 1 and 1.5. So the burst covers at most 1.5x its way (27.2 ->
  40.8 units).
- **On the ground:** the animation moves him, and the MSC adds (k - 1) of it forward through `0x0F000004`. Up, it
  moves him up through `0x0F000005` and sets SITUATION 2, so with Leave_Ground_Cancel off the move goes on in the air.
  Down, it never moves him, so he can't go into the floor.
- **In the air:** his kinetics move him. The MSC makes up the difference to the aimed path, measured from the frame
  before, and moves him down at most 3 a frame.
- **No target:** exactly v11.

### A faster startup on a combo
At the move's start (gf_init), if an opponent in hitstun is within 40 units, using gf_pick's rules (in front, within
60 degrees), the motion plays at 2x up to the punch (motion frame 14) and at 1x from then on.
- **The punch:** comes out on game frame 7 (normal: 14). The hit check / catch is at game frame 11 (normal: 18).
- **The rest:** everything is tied to motion frames, so it shifts with the punch.
- **Tracking:** still works, on the burst frames the motion plays at 2x (10, 12, 14), with twice the speed a frame.
