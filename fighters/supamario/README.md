# Supamario MSC

`supamario.c`: Supamario's decompiled MSC (first commit: the v5 package's `supamario.mscsb` as
decompiled; a copy of Mario's with the universal mechanics). Built by
`tools/supamario_kame_build.py`, which replaces the `KAME_*` names.

## Neutral special: Kamehameha (status 0x152)

`kame_n_*` (end of the file), called from the vanilla status functions: `func_2256` (main: init),
`func_2257` (per frame: `kame_n_loop`), `func_2258` (situation: re-arms the air stall), `func_2279`
(end: the charge effects off). Hold special to charge (the throw frozen at frame 10), release (or
100 frames) to fire article 5 at frame 14 in status 0 / 1 / 2 = the charge level; the firing pose
(frame 16) is held while the beam exists. In the air he floats down while charging and floats while
the beam is out. The fireball
article (0) is unchanged, not generated by this move. Details: `docs/custom-effects.md`, "The move".

## weapons/

- `kamehameha.c`: the Kamehameha projectile (weapon kind 0x18B): fly / hold / finish statuses.

## Kamehameha fix 85 (`build/supamario_kame_fix85.zip`)

User: move the beam a very small amount forward after the first hit (moving opponents still get hit), let it
multi-hit a shield instead of getting stuck where it was shielded, make it unaffected by staling, and make its
hitboxes linger until it begins to disappear. Install over fighters_latest (the Kamehameha files of test 30).

- **Creep:** the hold moves forward `KAME_CREEP` (0.2) a frame (was 0).
- **Shields:** the hit status (3) checks the fighters around the head (`kame_near`: 10 behind to 20 ahead of it,
  their feet 8 above to 28 below it; Supamario stays behind it): only shielding ones (status 0x1B-0x1E) means a
  shield: it flies on (`kame_shielded`, its fly motion again from its frame: the status change takes its
  hitboxes), multi-hitting the shield as it passes; a later connection (`AttackModule(0x2727)`, reset with
  `0x2724` while it's only shields) with a fighter not shielding starts the hold there.
- **Lingering hitbox:** the body capsule (ID 1) covers the whole beam: from the head back over all the ground it
  covered, every frame of the flight (`supamario_kame_build.py fly_game`; it was the last 4 frames' worth). The
  hold plays the fly motion from the frame it hit at, frozen (`kame_hold_begin`), so the capsule stays the beam's
  length (not proven in game: that starting a motion part-way runs its script up to there); the finish / the
  dissolve removes it.
- **Staling:** neutral special's status data (`func_2250`) had the attack log `0x11192` (the fireball's special
  N); now 0 (the note sheet's "stale move negation": 0 = off). Not proven that the beam's hits take their staling
  from it.
- Built without the XV2 package: the MSC from `kamehameha.c` with test 30's KAME_* values
  (`tools/supamario_kame_values.py`, read back from fighters_latest's file: the unchanged source compiles to it
  byte for byte) + `KAME_CREEP`; the weapon game.bin = fighters_latest's with fly1-3 from `fly_game` (its first 4
  capsules checked equal to the installed ones); `supamario.mscsb` = fighters_latest's with that one word 0.

## Kamehameha fix 89 (`build/supamario_kame_fix89.zip`)

Install over fix 88. User (clip): it still only multi-hits a shield fully charged.

- **Cause:** native, not the MSC. A weapon's hit result reaches its singleton's hit method (+0xAC) as bits; the
  base fireball's (0x0320EE2C) removes the weapon on 0x13 and on 0x4 (unless the weapon's byte +0x46 is set),
  and 0x4 is the shield-block result. The beam's own method (`weapon_expand.py` on_hit) caught only 0x13, so a
  shield block went to the base method and the beam was removed before its MSC saw it. Fully charged, the hit
  presumably came in as 0x13 (its damage; its status 3 and the fly-through on the shield ran), hence "only fully charged".
- **Fix:** `extra_kinds.json` kamehameha `"on_hit_bits": "0x17"` (`weapon_expand.py`'s new per-article option):
  a shield block asks for status 3 too, which (fix 85-88) flies on, multi-hitting the shield. One word in the
  RPX: 0x04BB43FC `andi. r0, r5, 0x13` -> `0x17` (`weapon_expand_test.py` checks the method on bit 0x4).
- Changed: `code/cross_f.rpx` only (fix 79's plus that word); the scripts are fix 88's.
